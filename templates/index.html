1. The Real Bottleneck

Student WhatsApp groups bury urgent sparks and sick students under trivial complaints. Wardens need an instant executive view.

2. Local Gemma 4 Engine

100% on-device via Ollama. No private student data or room details leave the campus network, with zero cloud API bills.

3. From Chat to Action

Generates a formatted WhatsApp summary for maintenance teams in 1 click and auto-drafts replies to students missing room numbers.
