Hostel Incident Protocols & Terms of Service

Official campus standard operating procedures, disciplinary provisions, SLA resolution matrices, and emergency room entry rights.

Section 1: Frivolous Complaints & Misuse Penalties

Strict Enforcement

Submitting fraudulent P0 emergency tickets or intentionally triggering false alarms will be classified as a Level 3 Disciplinary Offense under Section 14 of the University Code of Conduct. Penalties include a mandatory ₹5,000 fine and referral to the Chief Proctorial Board.

Section 2: SLA Response Matrices & Contractor Liability

Operational Policy
Severity Category Max Resolution Window Escalation Trigger
P0 (Critical) Fire, Gas Leak, Electrical Spark 15 Minutes Chief Warden Direct Alert
P1 (Urgent) Water Main Burst, Elevator Stuck 60 Minutes Duty Proctor Notification
P2 (Normal) Room Geyser, Wi-Fi Switch, Fan 4 Hours Maintenance Supervisor

Section 3: Emergency Room Entry Protocols

Life Safety

Hostel wardens and certified electrical/plumbing personnel reserve the right to enter occupied student rooms without prior written notice under active life-safety emergencies (active flooding, burning smell, gas odor). Dual-staff witness sign-off is logged automatically into the incident docket.

Section 4: Quiet Hours (22:00 - 06:00) Nocturnal Dispatch

Residential Welfare

Non-essential maintenance dispatches (P2 and P3) are paused during designated quiet hours (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) to maintain resident study and sleep environments. All P0 life-safety and security dispatches remain active 24/7/365 without exception.
